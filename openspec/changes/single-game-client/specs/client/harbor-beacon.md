# Canonical harbor client preservation

The sole /game client SHALL render the original harbor counters, contribution interaction and beacon from the canonical PlayerWorldSnapshot. It SHALL NOT map these counters to wallet/currency/items, fabricate unresolved values, maintain another room/account clock or award resources from an ACK.

Contribution SHALL use the existing admitted cookie client and empty contribute payload. The displayed self-account assertion SHALL remain required. Outstanding and unobserved accepted contributions SHALL block repeated submission. Disconnect, logout, replacement identity and obsolete owner epochs SHALL prevent stale callbacks from restoring client state or automatically retrying contribution.

The client SHALL display both ready progress and explicit null legacy-review-required progress. Null progress SHALL disable contribution. Same-region peer counters SHALL use only their canonical public progress.

Collection countdown SHALL use beacon.tick/closesAtTick/tickMs. The rendered beacon SHALL exist only in its server region, with its server position/radius, and SHALL NOT introduce client collision authority. Completed lighting, contributors and rewarded accounts SHALL come from authoritative snapshots.
