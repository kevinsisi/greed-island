# Prototype card artwork

Original illustrations generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-10-07. No external stock images or franchise artwork were used. The original PNGs remain in the repository's `packages/web/artwork/source/` directory. These JPEGs are delivery derivatives: 768 × 1152 full art and 256 × 384 thumbnails, encoded with macOS `sips`.

The React component supplies the frame; card names and gameplay text remain HTML in the HUD. No text is baked into the illustrations.

## Shared prompt direction

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait illustration for a premium fantasy island spell card, 2:3 aspect ratio. Hand painted gouache and intricate engraved ink linework, premium collectible card illustration. Strong readable central silhouette at thumbnail scale. Full bleed art edge to edge, no frame, absolutely no words letters numbers logos or watermarks. Original artwork, not imitating any existing franchise.

## 星火 / ember

Source: `packages/web/artwork/source/ember.png`.

Prompt subject: A single luminous ember blooms from a cracked ancient lantern held above an island forest shrine. The ember forms an elegant tiny phoenix made of curling fire, with layered curling feathers, scattered gold sparks and deep black-red shadows. Behind it: soft silhouettes of dense island foliage, moss covered carved basalt ruins, a few glowing flowers. Jewel-like but restrained, aged copper and amber against midnight forest green. Exquisite textured details on the lantern, dramatic warm illumination, intimate mythic mood.

## 回潮 / tide

Source: `packages/web/artwork/source/tide.png`.

Prompt subject: A healing tide ascends out of a giant pearlescent nautilus shell resting on an ancient island altar surrounded by tide pools. The translucent curl of turquoise water embraces a luminous pearl and delicate glowing lotus-like sea blossom, like a protective hand formed from water; silver koi silhouettes and suspended droplets circle through the arc. Behind: moonlit arch of eroded basalt and a still jade sea, lush fern details in the foreground. Elegant detailed organic materials, aged gold accents and pearl white against midnight forest green, cool pale jade illumination. Exquisite shell carving and watery caustic textures, peaceful restorative mythic mood.

## 渡風 / wind

Source: `packages/web/artwork/source/wind.png`.

Prompt subject: A weathered stone doorway floating on an island cliff is opening through the power of a luminous ivory feather. Pale jade wind ribbons spiral around the feather into a long suspended bridge of golden leaves and airborne stone fragments, reaching toward a distant ruined island temple. Two graceful swallows ride the currents, intricate ferns and small wildflowers on the foreground cliff, distant misty sea. Elegant detailed organic materials, aged gold accents and ivory against midnight forest green, atmospheric morning light through the doorway. Strong readable central feather-and-arch silhouette at thumbnail scale, exquisite weathered stone and fine feather textures, adventurous liberating mythic mood.
