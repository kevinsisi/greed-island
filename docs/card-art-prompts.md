# Greed Island — 100-Card Art Generation Prompts

**Base style (apply to every prompt):**
> Painterly dark fantasy trading card illustration, Hunter x Hunter Greed Island aesthetic, jewel tones, dramatic lighting, portrait orientation, no text, no card borders, no UI elements.

**Rank mood guide:**
- **S** — Legendary, mythic, ethereal glow, supreme power radiating
- **A** — Powerful and rare, vivid magical energy, commanding
- **B** — Skilled craftsmanship, warm confident tones
- **C** — Everyday magic, gentle charm
- **D** — Humble and weathered, worn texture, simple but real

**Category palette guide:**
| 分類 | 主色調 | 氛圍 |
|------|--------|------|
| 潮源系 | Deep ocean blue + ember orange | Lighthouse, tidal ruins, ancient runes, cobblestone harbor |
| 食飲系 | Warm amber + lantern gold | Night market steam, street food glow, festive warmth |
| 技藝系 | Steel grey + forge orange | Metalwork, weaving looms, pottery, craftsman tools |
| 地景系 | Dusk gold + sea mist teal | Panoramic vistas, sacred geography, ancient stones |
| 潮器系 | Cold steel + tidal shimmer | Weapons/armor with magical sheen, material close-up |
| 生靈系 | Bioluminescent green + twilight | Animals/creatures, natural spirits, living energy |
| 契約系 | Ink black + candlelight gold | Antique keys/seals, handwritten letters, ceremonial items |
| 秘聞系 | Dusty parchment + mystery purple | Ciphers, hidden maps, candle-lit manuscripts |
| 潮術系 | Electric teal + fire orange | Elemental magic bursts, technique visualization, energy flows |
| 深淵系 | Void black + abyssal blue | Underground caverns, eldritch glow, ancient deep darkness |
